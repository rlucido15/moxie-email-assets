Questions about your refinance benefit

What is the Moxie Client Benefit?
Clients whose current mortgage closed with Moxie Mortgage pay no Moxie lender fees (origination, processing and underwriting) when they refinance that home with Moxie. Terms apply; see disclosure 1.
Which fees does Moxie cover?
Moxie covers its own origination, processing and underwriting fees. Third-party and government fees, such as appraisal, credit report, title, settlement, recording fees and transfer taxes, plus prepaid interest, escrow deposits and any discount points you choose, are not covered.
How often can I use the benefit?
You can use it every time rates drop, after making at least 6 consecutive on-time payments on your current Moxie loan, for as long as you own the home. The benefit may change or end at any time, but changes won't affect a loan already locked.
How do I see if I can lower my payment?
Call 480.660.2518 or email hello@moxiemortgage.com. Because Moxie already has your file, we'll review your options within one business day, at no cost and with no obligation.

Disclosures

  1. Moxie Client Benefit. Available to borrowers whose current mortgage closed with Moxie Mortgage who refinance that home with Moxie Mortgage after making at least 6 consecutive on-time monthly payments on the current loan. Covered “lender fees” are Moxie’s origination, processing and underwriting fees only. Not included: third-party and government fees (such as appraisal, credit report, title, settlement, recording fees and transfer taxes), prepaid interest, escrow or impound deposits, and any discount points you choose to pay. Covered fees may be provided as a lender credit. All loans are subject to credit approval, underwriting guidelines, property eligibility and program requirements; not all applicants will qualify, and a refinance must provide a benefit to you. The benefit has no cash value, is not transferable and may be changed or discontinued at any time; changes will not affect a loan already locked.
