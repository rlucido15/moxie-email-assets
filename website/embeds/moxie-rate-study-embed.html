Methodology

How we compared

We didn’t pick favorable weeks. The study includes every rate sheet Moxie sent with a Rocket Mortgage comparison, and every rate is as published.

  • Source

    Rocket Mortgage’s rates were recorded exactly as quoted on its website at rocketmortgage.com/mortgage-rates. Moxie Mortgage’s rates were recorded matching the exact terms quoted, apples to apples.

  • Period

    98 weeks of rates, dated Apr 26, 2024 through Nov 5, 2026.

    Loan types

    Conventional, FHA and VA, giving 294 rate comparisons in all.

  • Scenario

    Rate comparisons were recorded matching the exact terms quoted by Rocket Mortgage that week, apples to apples: $275,000 loan, 740 credit score, 80% loan-to-value, 30-year fixed. Discount points varied by week, based on the costs Rocket Mortgage quoted that week.

  • Calculation

    The gap is Rocket Mortgage’s rate minus Moxie’s rate on each sheet. The headline figure is the average of all 294 gaps: 0.688%. The APR gap averaged 0.805%.

Questions

About the rate study

How much lower are Moxie Mortgage’s rates than Rocket Mortgage’s?

+
Across 98 Moxie rate sheets from April 2024 to November 2026, Moxie’s 30-year fixed rates averaged 0.688% lower than the Rocket Mortgage rates shown on the same sheets. By loan type, the average gap was 0.74% for conventional, 0.67% for FHA and 0.65% for VA loans.

Was Moxie lower every time?

+
Yes. Moxie’s rate was lower in all 294 comparisons in the study (98 rate sheets × 3 loan types). The smallest gap was 0.115% and the largest was 1.375%.

What loan scenario did the study compare?

+
Every rate sheet priced the same scenario for both lenders: a $275,000 30-year fixed loan, a 740 credit score and 80% loan-to-value, for conventional, FHA and VA loans.

How much could a 0.688% lower rate save me?

+
On the study’s $275,000 loan, the average gap worked out to about $122 a month in principal and interest, $1,464 a year and $7,322 over five years. Savings scale with your loan amount and depend on your own rate, credit and loan.

Has the gap changed over time?

+
The gap was widest in 2024 (0.86% on average), 0.76% in 2025 and 0.51% so far in 2026. Moxie stayed lower on every sheet in every year.

Why can a mortgage broker like Moxie offer lower rates?

+
Moxie Mortgage is a mortgage broker that works directly with wholesale lenders. Wholesale pricing skips much of the branch and marketing overhead built into retail lenders’ rates, and Moxie can compare more than 300 wholesale investors to find the best price for each borrower.
