Is refinancing worth it?

Usually, if your current rate is 0.5%–1% or more above today's rates. Refinancing can also make sense to switch from an ARM to a fixed rate, drop mortgage insurance, consolidate high-interest debt, or pull cash from your equity.

Refinance closing costs typically run 2%–5% of the loan amount and can often be rolled into the new loan. Qualifying borrowers who bought their home with Moxie can refinance at no cost.

What you need to refinance

  1. Sufficient home equity
  2. A solid credit score (620+ for most lenders)
  3. Stable income and a low debt-to-income ratio
  4. On-time payment history

Refinance questions, answered

Browse every mortgage FAQ

Will I need another appraisal when refinancing?

+

In most cases, lenders require an appraisal to confirm the current market value of the property; however, FHA Streamline and VA IRRRL refinances generally do not require an appraisal.

Can I roll refinance closing costs into my loan?

+

Yes, often closing costs can be financed into the new loan, reducing your upfront out-of-pocket expenses.

What is a streamline refinance?

+

A streamline refinance is a simplified process available for current FHA or VA loan holders, which does not require an appraisal.

Does Moxie offer no-cost refinance?

+

Yes, for qualifying borrowers we offer a no-cost refinances for life program, which can save clients $1,000s in origination fees and unlock financing strategies that otherwise would not be feasible.
