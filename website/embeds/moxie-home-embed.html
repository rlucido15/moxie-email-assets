What is a wholesale mortgage?

A wholesale mortgage is like an insider price for your home loan. Big banks and retail lenders add markups to pay for offices, managers, and ads. Moxie skips the retail markup, finds the best deal for you, and puts those savings back in your pocket.

How much can you save with a wholesale lender?

+

You can save on both closing costs and your monthly payment. Retail lenders often charge 4.5% to 6% for your loan, and Moxie charges a fraction of that. On a $500,000 mortgage, a 3% difference in margin could mean $15,000 in closing costs, or a lower rate that saves $10,000s over the life of the loan.

Is it faster to get a mortgage through a wholesale lender?

+

Yes. Wholesale lenders like Moxie skip the red tape and long turn times of large retail banks.

Do wholesale lenders have more loan options than big banks?

+

Yes. A retail bank limits you to its own products and pricing. Moxie borrowers get access to 300+ lenders that compete for your business, so you get the best product at the best market price.

What is a correspondent lender?

+

A correspondent lender funds its own mortgage loans and partners with large wholesale investors who assume the loan after closing. It gives Moxie clients the full range of loan options under one roof, with a streamlined approval process.
